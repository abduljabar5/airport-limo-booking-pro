Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you have a car at my door? +

Online bookings need two hours of notice. For sooner than that, call dispatch and we will tell you honestly what is available. Same-day airport runs are usually possible outside peak holiday travel.

Can I change or cancel a booking? +

Yes. Call or reply to your confirmation text or email. Cancellations are free up to 24 hours before pickup, and changes to time, address or vehicle are handled by dispatch at no charge.

Do you offer corporate accounts? +

Yes. Call or email and ask for a corporate account. We set up saved travelers, monthly invoicing and a dedicated contact, usually the same day.

I need more than six seats. Who do I call? +

Dispatch. Groups above six are handled by sending two or three vehicles together with one point of contact. Give us the headcount, luggage and schedule and we will build the plan.

Will I get a text from my chauffeur? +

Yes, when you check the SMS consent box at booking. You receive a confirmation, a reminder before pickup and a message when your chauffeur is staged. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
