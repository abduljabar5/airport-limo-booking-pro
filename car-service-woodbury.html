The east metro

Woodbury to MSP, on the east side of the river

Woodbury is the largest city on the east side of the metro, and for most of its neighborhoods the drive to MSP is about 25 minutes on I-494 south across the Mississippi. We pick up in the subdivisions around Colby Lake, Carver Lake, Bailey Lake and Wedgewood, along Radio Drive and Valley Creek Road, and in the newer developments on the south side toward Cottage Grove. Your fare is quoted flat before you confirm, and your flight is tracked from the moment you book.

Woodbury is also the commercial heart of the east metro. We drop at Woodbury Lakes, Tamarack Village and CityPlace, meet guests at the Sheraton, the Hilton Garden Inn, the Residence Inn and the other hotels off I-94 and Radio Drive, and serve the corporate offices along Hudson Road and the I-94 corridor for visiting executives on account. Woodwinds Hospital and the medical buildings around it are regular stops for patients and visiting physicians.

Beyond the airport, Woodbury riders use us for nights out in downtown Saint Paul and Minneapolis, Wild games at the Xcel Energy Center, and the occasional ride to Rochester for a Mayo Clinic appointment. Whatever the trip, dispatch answers the phone at any hour, the chauffeur helps with every bag, and a round trip booked together saves 10 percent on the pair.

Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get from Woodbury to MSP? +

About 25 minutes from most of Woodbury via I-494 south across the river, or a little longer from the far east side near Afton. Rush hour on I-94 and 494 can add time, so we schedule pickups with a cushion and monitor traffic before we leave.

Do you pick up at Woodbury Lakes and the hotels off I-94? +

Yes. Woodbury Lakes, Tamarack Village, CityPlace and the Sheraton, Hilton Garden Inn, Residence Inn and other hotels along I-94 and Radio Drive are regular stops. Tell us the store, restaurant or hotel entrance and the chauffeur will be there.

What do fares from Woodbury to the airport start at? +

Fares from Woodbury start at $49 in a Lincoln Continental sedan, $59 in a Mercedes-Benz S-Class executive sedan and $69 in a Cadillac Escalade SUV, plus distance. The flat fare is shown before you confirm and never surges.

Do you serve the east metro beyond Woodbury? +

Yes. We cover Oakdale, Lake Elmo, Cottage Grove, Afton, Stillwater and the rest of Washington County, and we serve Hudson, Wisconsin across the river as well. Any address east of Saint Paul is a normal ride for us.

Do you handle early morning pickups in Woodbury? +

Every day. A 4 AM pickup in Woodbury for a 6 AM flight is routine. Dispatch runs 24/7, and the chauffeur texts when the car is in your driveway. Book online two or more hours ahead, or call for sooner.

Is a round trip from Woodbury to MSP discounted? +

Yes. Booking the departure and the return together saves 10 percent on the pair. The return is tracked against your inbound flight, with 30 minutes of complimentary waiting after a domestic landing, and the same chauffeur drives both legs whenever the schedule allows.

Can you take me to Woodwinds Hospital and wait during an appointment? +

Yes. Book point to point each way, or hourly with a three-hour minimum so the same chauffeur waits at Woodwinds Hospital or the nearby clinics and drives you home. Chauffeurs help with walkers and bags and drop you at the entrance you name. The price is quoted before you confirm.

Can I book a car from Woodbury to Rochester for Mayo Clinic? +

Yes. Woodbury to Mayo Clinic is about 90 minutes on I-494 and Highway 52, quoted as one flat fare before you confirm. The round trip saves 10 percent, returns are timed around your last appointment, and the Cadillac Escalade is our recommendation for Highway 52 in winter.

Can a Woodbury company set up a corporate account? +

Yes. Offices along Hudson Road, the I-94 corridor and CityPlace use us for executive and visitor transportation on accounts with one monthly invoice, saved travelers and receipts for every ride. Call dispatch or use the corporate transportation page to get started, usually the same day.

Can you take us to a Wild game at the Xcel Energy Center and pick us up after? +

Yes. Downtown Saint Paul is about 15 minutes from Woodbury on I-94. Your chauffeur drops you at the Xcel Energy Center door and meets you afterward at an agreed spot on West Seventh, away from the crowd. The flat fare does not surge on game nights, and a round trip saves 10 percent.
