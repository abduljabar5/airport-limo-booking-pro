About Total Town Car

Your trusted partner for premium Limo, Black Car, and Airport Taxi service in Minneapolis.

Our Story

Founded on the principles of reliability, luxury, and unparalleled customer service, Total Town Car Service has grown from a passionate idea into Minneapolis's most trusted transportation provider. We saw a need for a dependable, high-end limo service that catered to the discerning needs of residents, business travelers, and visitors. Whether you need a sophisticated black car for a corporate event or a timely airport taxi to MSP, our mission has always been to exceed expectations.

Our journey began with a single vehicle and a simple promise: to provide a seamless and luxurious travel experience. Today, we boast a diverse fleet of premium vehicles and a team of professional chauffeurs dedicated to upholding our founding values. We are proud to be a leading provider of airport limo services, connecting the Twin Cities with the world, one comfortable ride at a time.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an exceptional transportation service that is safe, reliable, and luxurious. We aim to be more than just a taxi service; we are your personal chauffeurs, committed to making your journey as smooth and stress-free as possible. From the moment you book your black car service to your final destination, we handle every detail with precision and care.

We are dedicated to setting a new standard for ground transportation in the Twin Cities. This means investing in the latest technology for seamless booking and communication, maintaining our vehicles to the highest safety standards, and continuously training our team to anticipate and meet your needs.
