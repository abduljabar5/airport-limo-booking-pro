About Total Town Car Service

Our Story: Minneapolis's Premier Limo Service

Founded on the principles of reliability, luxury, and unparalleled customer service, Total Town Car Service has grown from a passionate idea into Minneapolis's most trusted transportation provider. We saw a need for a dependable, high-end limo service that catered to the discerning needs of residents, business travelers, and visitors. Whether you need a sophisticated black car for a corporate event or a timely airport taxi to MSP, our mission has always been to exceed expectations.

Our journey began with a single vehicle and a simple promise: to provide a seamless and luxurious travel experience. Today, we boast a diverse fleet of premium vehicles and a team of professional chauffeurs dedicated to upholding our founding values. We are proud to be a leading provider of airport limo services, connecting the Twin Cities with the world, one comfortable ride at a time.

Our Mission: Redefining Transportation

Our mission is to provide an exceptional transportation service that is safe, reliable, and luxurious. We aim to be more than just a taxi service; we are your personal chauffeurs, committed to making your journey as smooth and stress-free as possible. From the moment you book your black car service to your final destination, we handle every detail with precision and care. This commitment extends to every airport limo and airport taxi ride, ensuring on-time performance for every departure and arrival at MSP.

We are dedicated to setting a new standard for ground transportation in the Twin Cities. This means investing in the latest technology for seamless booking and communication, maintaining our vehicles to the highest safety standards, and continuously training our team to anticipate and meet your needs. Our goal is to make every journey with us a memorable and positive experience, reinforcing our reputation as the go-to limo service in Minneapolis.

Our Fleet: The Pinnacle of Luxury

A superior travel experience begins with a superior vehicle. Our fleet represents the best in luxury, comfort, and reliability. Unlike a standard taxi, each vehicle in our collection is a late-model, high-end option chosen for its performance and amenities.

  • Executive Taxis: The cornerstone of our black car service, perfect for business travel, solo trips, and elegant evenings out.
  • Luxury SUVs: Ideal for small groups, families needing extra luggage space, or those who prefer a more commanding presence on the road. A perfect choice for an MSP airport limo run.
  • Stretch Limousines: For weddings, proms, and other special occasions, our stretch limos provide the ultimate statement of style and luxury.
  • Executive Vans: When you need to transport a larger group for a corporate outing or family event, our vans offer comfort without sacrificing style.

Every vehicle is subject to a rigorous maintenance schedule and is professionally detailed daily. Your safety and comfort are paramount, which is why our cars are equipped with premium sound systems, climate control, and plush interiors to make your ride exceptional.

Our Chauffeurs: The Heart of Our Service

While our vehicles are top-of-the-line, it's our chauffeurs who truly define the Total Town Car experience. They are more than just drivers; they are professionally trained, licensed, and insured experts in the art of chauffeuring. Each member of our team is selected for their professionalism, punctuality, and deep knowledge of the Minneapolis-St. Paul area.
