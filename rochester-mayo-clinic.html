Rochester Transportation FAQs

How long does it take to get from MSP to Rochester?

The drive from MSP Airport to Rochester/Mayo Clinic takes approximately 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes depending on traffic and weather conditions.

Can the driver wait during my appointment?

Yes, we offer wait-time service for an additional hourly fee. This is ideal for same-day round trips. Alternatively, you can schedule a pickup time for after your appointment.

What if my appointment runs long?

We understand medical appointments can be unpredictable. Just text or call your driver with updates. We're flexible and will adjust pickup times as needed.

Do you provide wheelchair-accessible vehicles?

Our standard SUVs can accommodate folding wheelchairs and walkers. For specialized wheelchair-accessible vehicles, please contact us in advance to arrange appropriate transportation.
