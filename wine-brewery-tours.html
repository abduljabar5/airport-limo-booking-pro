Frequently Asked Questions

How many people can you accommodate?

We can accommodate groups of 1-6 passengers in our luxury sedans and SUVs. For larger groups, we can arrange multiple vehicles or recommend partner services with larger capacity vehicles.

Can we bring bottles we purchase back in the car?

Absolutely! We provide coolers and bags to safely transport your wine and beer purchases. Your chauffeur will help load and unload your bottles.

What if we want to change our itinerary during the tour?

Flexibility is built into our service. Your chauffeur is happy to adjust the route based on your preferences. Just let them know where you'd like to go next!

Do you provide route recommendations?

Yes! We're happy to suggest popular wine trails and brewery circuits based on your starting location and preferences. Just mention your interests when booking.

What's included in the hourly rate?

Our hourly rate includes the vehicle, professional chauffeur, fuel, bottled water, and all gratuity. The only additional costs would be tasting fees and food at your stops.
