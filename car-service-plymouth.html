Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get from Plymouth to MSP Airport? +

About 30 minutes from central Plymouth in normal traffic, via I-494 south or Highway 55 and I-35W. During the morning commute it can run closer to 40, which is why we schedule your pickup with a cushion and watch traffic before we leave.

Do you serve the corporate campuses along Highway 55 and I-494? +

Yes. Executive and visitor transportation for the Plymouth headquarters along Highway 55, Xenium Lane, Fernbrook Lane and the 494 corridor is a big part of our week. We can set up an account with a single monthly invoice for your company.

What do fares from Plymouth to the airport start at? +

Fares from Plymouth start at $49 in a Lincoln Continental sedan, $59 in a Mercedes-Benz S-Class executive sedan and $69 in a Cadillac Escalade SUV, plus distance. You see the full flat fare before you confirm, and it never surges.

Can you pick up my parents at MSP and bring them to Plymouth? +

Of course. Give us their flight number and we track it, wait 30 minutes after a domestic landing at no charge, and can add a Meet & Greet with a name sign at baggage claim for $15 so they are never looking for the car.

Do you handle early morning pickups in Plymouth? +

Yes, at any hour. Dispatch runs 24/7 and 3:30 or 4 AM pickups for the first flights are routine from the northwest metro. Book online at least two hours in advance or call for anything sooner.
