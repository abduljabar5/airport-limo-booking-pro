Wedding Transportation FAQs

How far in advance should I book for my wedding?

We recommend booking 2-3 months in advance, especially for peak wedding season (May-October) and Saturday dates. However, we can sometimes accommodate last-minute requests.

Can the vehicle be decorated?

We provide complimentary "Just Married" signs and ribbon decorations. If you have specific decoration requests, please discuss them with us in advance. We ask that no tape or adhesives be used on the vehicle exterior.

What if our schedule changes on the wedding day?

We understand weddings can run behind schedule. Our chauffeurs are flexible and will wait as needed. We recommend building buffer time into your transportation schedule and keeping our driver informed of any changes.

Do you coordinate with wedding planners?

Absolutely! We're happy to work directly with your wedding planner or coordinator to ensure seamless logistics. We can provide them with driver contact information and vehicle details in advance.
